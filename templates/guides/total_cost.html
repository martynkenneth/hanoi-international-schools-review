What one-time fees should I budget for?

Most families underestimate the upfront costs of joining an international school. Plan for these one-off payments:

  • Application fee: $150–$500 (non-refundable)
  • Enrolment deposit: $1,000–$5,000 (usually deducted from first term's fees)
  • Capital levy / building fund: $500–$3,000 at some schools (one-time, non-refundable)
  • Uniform starter pack: $200–$600 (varies significantly by school)
  • Technology levy: $200–$500/year at some schools for device programmes

How fast do international school fees increase?

Hanoi international school fees typically increase by 4–8% per year. Over a 5-year secondary school programme, this compounds significantly. A school with $20,000 tuition in Year 7 could cost $27,000–$29,000 by Year 12 at that rate.

Budget tip: Ask each school for their fee increase history over the past 5 years before making a long-term commitment. Some schools are more predictable than others.

How can I reduce the total cost?

  • Apply for scholarships: Multiple Hanoi schools offer merit scholarships covering 5–30% of fees. See our active offers page.
  • Ask about sibling discounts: BIS (10%), VAS (15%), and others offer discounts for families with two or more children enrolled.
  • Take early-enrolment discounts: Several schools offer 5–10% off if you confirm and pay before a deadline. See our promotions page.
  • Live closer to the school: Transport is a major variable cost. Living within the school's free bus zone or close enough to walk/cycle saves $1,200–$2,400/year.
  • Compare total costs, not just tuition: A school with $2,000 lower tuition but expensive transport and activity fees may not be cheaper overall.

Frequently asked questions about international school fees in Hanoi

Are fees quoted per year or per term?

Most Hanoi international schools quote annual fees. Invoicing is typically per term (2 or 3 terms per year). Some schools offer a small discount for annual payment in full.

Are enrollment deposits refundable if we don't proceed?

Policies vary by school. Some deposits are fully deductible from first term fees; others are partly or fully non-refundable if you withdraw. Always ask before paying.

Do fees include Vietnamese language tuition?

At most fully English-medium international schools, Vietnamese language is included as a compulsory or elective subject within the tuition fee. At bilingual schools (Wellspring, Olympia, etc.), it is core to the curriculum and included.

Are there hidden fees I should ask about?

Ask specifically about: technology device programme fees, subject-specific material charges, residential trip levies, IB exam registration fees (typically $200–400/subject), and any capital development funds.
