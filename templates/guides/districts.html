Frequently asked questions about Hanoi school districts

Which district is best for expat families in Hanoi?

Tay Ho (West Lake) is the most popular expat district — it has the largest expat community, the most international restaurants and services, and the highest concentration of international schools. Long Bien (especially Vinhomes Riverside) is the second most popular for families who prefer a quieter, more suburban environment.

Should I choose my apartment based on the school location or vice versa?

Generally, choose the school first. The school is the bigger long-term commitment and the harder thing to change. Most Hanoi expat districts have good housing options, so you can usually find a suitable apartment near your preferred school.

How bad is the traffic in Hanoi for the school run?

Rush hour traffic (7–9am, 5–7pm) in Hanoi can be severe, especially on routes between Tay Ho and Long Bien, or crossing the Red River bridges. If your child uses the school bus, the bus route is designed to account for this. For car or motorbike pickup, budget an extra 30–50% on journey times during rush hour.

Are there international schools in the Ha Dong or southern districts?

Yes — SIS Gamuda Gardens, Reigate Grammar School Vietnam, and ParkCity International School are all in the Ha Dong / Gamuda area. These suit families living in the south of the city.
